# Dashboard Review Notes

## What Works Well

- Clear KPI cards at the top of each page.
- Separate pages for executive summary, customer analytics and financial analytics.
- Good use of ranked bar charts for regions, categories, products and customers.
- Filters make the report interactive by year, region and customer type.
- The financial page separates revenue, profit, cost and margin, which supports better business interpretation.

## Portfolio Strengths

- The project demonstrates end-to-end BI thinking: dataset, model, measures, report pages and business insights.
- The screenshots make the result visible without requiring Power BI Desktop.
- The report covers both commercial and financial performance, which makes it relevant for BI, sales analytics and financial analytics roles.

## Recommended Visual Polish

- Standardize labels to one language across visual titles, axes and slicers.
- Hide scrollbars by resizing visuals or reducing displayed category count.
- Format currency and unit labels consistently across pages.
- Add short tooltip explanations for KPI cards.
- Align slicers and page navigation elements consistently across report pages.

## Recommended Analytical Additions

- Period-over-period revenue and profit growth.
- Contribution to revenue/profit by category and region.
- Average order value.
- Profit per customer.
- Customer segment revenue share.
- Drillthrough pages for top customers and top products.

## Reproducibility Improvement

The repository would be stronger with exported CSV summaries or a data dictionary preview in Markdown. That would let reviewers inspect model inputs without opening the Excel workbook or Power BI file.
